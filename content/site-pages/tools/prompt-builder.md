## Why prompts matter

AI chatbots answer the question you **asked**, not the one you **meant**. A vague prompt like "explain loops" gets a long, generic answer. A clear one gets exactly what you need. This builder walks you through the six parts that make a prompt work, and scores it as you type.

## The six parts of a strong prompt

| Part | What it does | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Role | Sets the voice and level | "You are a friendly Python teacher." |
| Task | One clear action | "Explain list comprehensions." |
| Context | What the AI can't guess | "The student already knows for-loops." |
| Example | Shows the style you want | `[n * n for n in range(5)]` |
| Format | Shape of the answer | "3 bullet points and one code block." |
| Constraints | Tone, length, limits | "Simple English, under 150 words." |

You don't need all six every time. For a quick question, task and format are often enough. For anything you will reuse, fill in all six.

## How the score works

The builder counts how many parts you filled in and checks for vague words such as "good", "stuff" or "something" in your task. Five or six specific parts give a **strong** prompt.

## Use it safely

- Don't paste passwords, OTPs, Aadhaar or bank details into any AI tool.
- Check facts, numbers and code before you use them: AI can be confidently wrong.
