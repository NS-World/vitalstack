You don't need a computer science degree to work with AI. Commerce students, teachers, marketers, HR folks and shop owners are all learning it right now. This path starts from zero, uses everyday examples (UPI, cricket scores, exam marks) and gets you to a working AI app in about 8 weeks at **one hour a day**.

**How to use this page:** do one lesson a day, take its quiz, and tick it off. Your progress bars below fill up as you go.

## Week 1: Use AI tools like a pro

Before writing any code, learn to talk to AI well. Clear prompts, examples, roles and step-by-step thinking will help you at work from day one.

[[path:prompt-engineering]]

**Try this:** ask an AI to plan your week, explain a topic you find hard, and then check one of its answers yourself.
