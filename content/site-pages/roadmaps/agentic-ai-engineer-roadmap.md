AI agents plan, use tools, remember and work together to finish real tasks. Companies need engineers who can build them **reliably and safely**, and that's still a rare skill. This roadmap takes you from your first 40-line agent to production agents with evals, tracing, permissions and sandboxes. Plan on **3 months at 1–2 hours a day** if you know Python.

## What interviewers ask agent engineers

- "When would you NOT use an agent?" (Answer with the workflow-vs-agent test.)
- "How do you know your agent works?" (End-state evals, pass^k, tracing.)
- "How do you stop prompt injection from causing harm?" (Least privilege, taint tracking, approvals, sandboxes.)
- "How do you control cost?" (Routing, caching, step limits.)

Stages 2–6 are part of the **Agentic AI Engineer premium track**.
