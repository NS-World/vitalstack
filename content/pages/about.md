## Why VitalStack exists

When I started learning to code, most tutorials either assumed I already
knew things I didn't, or buried the useful part under pages of theory. I
spent a lot of time stuck on small problems that one clear sentence would
have solved.

VitalStack is the site I wish I'd had then: **plain-language guides to
programming and AI, in the order you should learn them**, with examples
you can actually run.

## Who it's for

- Students and beginners learning their first programming language
- People switching careers into tech
- Anyone who wants to use AI tools properly instead of guessing

## Our promise

- **No jargon without an explanation.**
- **Every code example is tested** before it's published.
- **Honest about limits.** If something is hard, outdated or not worth
  learning yet, we say so.
- **Kept current.** Guides are updated when tools change, and the
  "Updated" date at the top of each article tells you when.
