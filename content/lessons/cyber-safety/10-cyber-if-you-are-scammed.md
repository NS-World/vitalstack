Anyone can be scammed, including careful, educated and technical people. Scammers are professionals who do this all day. If it happens, **don't waste time on embarrassment**: fast action can freeze stolen money before it disappears. Keep this checklist somewhere your family can find it.

## If you lost money

1. **Call 1930 immediately.** This is India's National Cyber Crime Helpline. The sooner you call, the better the chance the money can be frozen.
2. **Report on cybercrime.gov.in** ("Report other cyber crime" → financial fraud). Note your complaint number.
3. **Call your bank** on its official number: block cards and UPI, and raise a fraud dispute. Ask for a reference number.
4. **Write down everything** while you remember: time, amount, transaction IDs, UPI IDs, phone numbers, website links.

## If an account was taken over

1. Use the platform's official **"Hacked account" or recovery** page (Google, Meta, WhatsApp, etc.).
2. Change the password and **turn on two-step verification**.
3. Log out of all other devices (security settings).
4. Check the recovery email and phone number: attackers often change them.
5. Warn friends and family that messages from the account may be fake.

## Evidence to keep

- Screenshots of chats, messages, call logs and payment screens.
- The scammer's phone numbers, UPI IDs, bank account numbers, websites and social profiles.
- Emails with full headers, if relevant.

Don't delete chats or block numbers **before** taking screenshots.

## Other places to report

| What | Where |
|---|---|
| Fraud calls, SMS and WhatsApp messages | Sancharsaathi portal (Chakshu) |
| Unknown mobile numbers on your ID | Sancharsaathi (Know your mobile connections) |
| Lost or stolen phone | Sancharsaathi (CEIR) and police |
| Cyber security incidents for organisations | CERT-In |
| Fake apps | Report inside the Play Store / App Store |

## Beware of "recovery" scams

After a fraud, you may get calls offering to "recover your money" for a fee, sometimes pretending to be police or lawyers. Real police don't charge recovery fees. Work only through 1930, the portal, your bank and your local police station.

## Look after yourself

Losing money to a scam is stressful and embarrassing, but it is a crime done **to** you, not a failure **by** you. Talk to family, share your story to warn others, and use what you learned in this course to protect them.

::tip Save "1930 Cyber Crime Helpline" in every family member's phone contacts today. In a panic, nobody remembers numbers.

## Summary

- Lost money? Call 1930 immediately, report on cybercrime.gov.in, and call your bank.
- Account hacked? Recover it officially, change the password, enable 2FA, sign out other devices.
- Keep evidence before blocking or deleting anything.
- Avoid "recovery" scams; real help never charges a fee.
