UPI made payments instant and easy, and scammers love instant and easy too. The good news: almost every UPI scam breaks one simple rule. Learn the rule and a few tricks, and you are safer than most people.

## The golden rule

> **You never need to enter your UPI PIN or scan a QR code to RECEIVE money.**

If anyone asks you to enter a PIN, approve a request or scan a code "to receive" a payment, it's a scam. A PIN is only for sending money out.

## The common UPI tricks

| Scam | How it works | Your defence |
|---|---|---|
| **Collect request** | The scammer sends a "request money" that looks like an incoming payment | Read the screen: "Pay" means money leaves you. Decline |
| **QR code** | "Scan this to receive your refund/prize" | Scanning is for paying. Never scan to receive |
| **Fake screenshot** | A fake "payment successful" image at your shop | Check your bank app or speaker device, not their phone |
| **"Sent by mistake"** | "I sent ₹5,000 by mistake, please return it" with a fake SMS | Check your actual balance. Ask them to contact their bank |
| **Screen-sharing app** | A fake "customer care" asks you to install AnyDesk/TeamViewer-like apps | Never install remote apps for anyone who called you |
| **Fake customer care** | A search result shows a fake helpline number | Get numbers only from the official app or website |

## OTP fraud

An OTP (one-time password) is the final lock on a transaction. That's why scammers want it so badly. They'll pretend to be your bank ("to stop a suspicious transaction"), a delivery agent ("to confirm your parcel"), or a friend ("I sent my OTP to your number by mistake").

**No genuine person needs your OTP.** Read the full OTP message: it usually says what it's for, like "OTP for payment of ₹19,999 to XYZ". That line alone exposes most scams.

## Settings that protect you

- **Set a daily UPI limit** in your app that matches your real needs.
- **Turn on transaction alerts** by SMS and app notifications, and actually read them.
- **Lock your UPI apps** with a phone lock and app lock.
- **Use a separate account** with a small balance for daily UPI if you can, and keep savings elsewhere.
- **Never save your UPI PIN** in notes or messages.

## If money is taken

Time matters. Act in the first hour:

1. Call **1930** (National Cyber Crime Helpline) and report at **cybercrime.gov.in**.
2. Call your bank's official number and ask them to block the account or card and raise a dispute.
3. Keep screenshots of messages, the scammer's UPI ID and number, and the transaction ID.

Under RBI rules, the faster you report an unauthorised transaction, the less you may have to bear, especially if you report within three working days.

::tip Teach this one line to every family member: "PIN means paying, never receiving." It stops the most common UPI scam in India.

## Summary

- You never need a PIN or QR scan to receive money.
- Decline unexpected collect requests; verify payments in your own bank app.
- Never share OTPs or install screen-sharing apps for callers.
- Set limits and alerts, and report fraud on 1930 within the first hour.
