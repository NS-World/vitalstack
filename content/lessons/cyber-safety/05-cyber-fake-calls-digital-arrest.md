**Social engineering** means manipulating people instead of hacking machines. The most powerful tool is the phone: a confident voice, a uniform on a video call, a threat of arrest. The scam works only while you stay on the call and stay scared. Your best defence is to hang up and verify.

## "Digital arrest" is not real

Police, CBI, customs, the RBI or courts **never** arrest anyone on a video call, never keep you on a call for hours, and never ask you to transfer money to "verify" or "clear" your name. If someone says you are under "digital arrest", it is a scam, every time.

## The common call scams

| Scam | The story | The tell |
|---|---|---|
| Courier / customs | "Illegal items found in your parcel" | They pass you to fake "police" and ask for money |
| Fake relative | "Your son is in police custody, send money now" | Unknown number, extreme urgency, secrecy |
| Fake bank / KYC | "Your account will be frozen today" | They ask for OTP, PIN or a remote app |
| Electricity / gas | "Your connection will be cut tonight" | A personal mobile number to "settle" the bill |
| Job / task scams | "Earn by liking videos or rating hotels" | Small payouts first, then you must "invest" to continue |
| Investment / trading | "Guaranteed 3% a day" WhatsApp groups | Fake profit screens, can't withdraw |
| Sextortion | A video call records you, then blackmail | Strangers who quickly want a video call |

## The psychology they use

- **Authority:** uniforms, official logos, legal words.
- **Fear:** arrest, account blocked, family in danger.
- **Urgency:** "right now", "don't hang up", "don't tell anyone".
- **Isolation:** they tell you to stay on the call and not talk to family. That alone is a huge red flag.

## Your 4-line safety script

1. "I'll call you back on the official number." (Then hang up.)
2. Look up the official number yourself, never the one they give you.
3. Talk to a family member or friend before doing anything with money.
4. Report the number on **Sancharsaathi (Chakshu)** and, if money is involved, call **1930**.

Real officials don't mind you calling back. Scammers do.

## Protecting older family members

- Agree on a **family code word** for real emergencies.
- Tell them: "No real officer asks for money on the phone. Call me first."
- Turn on spam call filtering on their phone, and save important numbers (bank, family) with clear names.

::tip If a call makes you scared or rushed, that feeling itself is your signal. Hang up first, think second. You lose nothing by calling back on an official number.

## Summary

- Social engineering uses authority, fear, urgency and isolation.
- "Digital arrest" does not exist; real agencies never take money on calls.
- Hang up, verify on official numbers, and talk to someone you trust.
- Report scam numbers on Sancharsaathi and money fraud on 1930.
