Social media apps are free because **your attention is the product**. The more time you spend, the more ads they can show, so the apps are carefully designed to keep you scrolling. That's not a conspiracy; it's the business model. Once you see the design tricks, they lose much of their power over you.

## The attention business

| You get | The app gets |
|---|---|
| Free entertainment, news and chat | Your time and attention, sold to advertisers |
| Recommendations you enjoy | Data about what keeps you watching |
| Likes and comments | A reason for you to come back again and again |

More minutes means more ads and more data, so every design choice is tested to increase time spent.

## Design tricks you can spot

- **Infinite scroll:** no natural stopping point, unlike the last page of a newspaper.
- **Autoplay:** the next video starts before you decide to watch it.
- **Variable rewards:** you never know if the next swipe brings something amazing, the same pull that makes slot machines addictive.
- **Notifications:** red badges and buzzes pull you back in ("someone liked your photo").
- **Streaks and counters:** fear of losing a streak keeps you opening the app daily.
- **Pull to refresh:** a small gesture with a surprise result, again like a slot machine lever.

## Why it works on everyone

These patterns use normal human psychology: curiosity, the desire for approval, and fear of missing out. Feeling hooked doesn't mean you're weak; it means the design is working as intended. Teenagers can be especially affected, which is why many experts recommend extra care with screen time for younger users.

## Taking back control

1. **Turn off non-essential notifications.** Keep only messages from real people.
2. **Use the built-in timers:** Digital Wellbeing (Android) or Screen Time (iPhone).
3. **Move social apps off the home screen** so opening them is a choice, not a reflex.
4. **No phone in the bedroom** an hour before sleep: use a cheap alarm clock.
5. **Use the "Following" feed** instead of the endless "For you" feed when the app allows it.
6. **Ask one question before opening an app:** "What am I here for?"

::tip Check your screen time for the last week right now. Most people are surprised. Pick one app and set a daily limit 30 minutes lower than your average.

## Summary

- Free apps earn money from your attention, so they're designed to hold it.
- Infinite scroll, autoplay, variable rewards, notifications and streaks keep you hooked.
- Timers, fewer notifications and phone-free bedtimes put you back in charge.
