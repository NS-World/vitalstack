You don't need to delete every app to have a healthier relationship with social media. Small, deliberate changes add up. This 7-day plan takes about 15 minutes a day and puts you back in charge of your time, attention and data.

## The plan

| Day | Task (about 15 minutes) |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Audit:** open Digital Wellbeing / Screen Time. Write down your top 3 apps and daily averages. |
| 2 | **Notifications:** turn off everything except messages from real people. |
| 3 | **Clean your feed:** unfollow or mute accounts that make you anxious, angry or envious; follow 5 that teach you something. |
| 4 | **Privacy:** private profile, hide phone number, location tags off, contact sync off (see the Cyber Safety course). |
| 5 | **Limits:** set daily app limits about 30% below your average; move social apps off the home screen. |
| 6 | **Replace:** pick a better default for idle moments: a book, a walk, a podcast, a lesson on this site. |
| 7 | **Review:** compare screen time with Day 1. Keep what worked, adjust what didn't. |

## Rules that make it stick

- **One screen at a time:** no scrolling while studying or eating.
- **No phone for the first 30 minutes after waking and the last 60 before sleep.**
- **Create more than you consume:** write, build, learn, share something useful.
- **Weekly check-in:** 5 minutes every Sunday to look at your numbers.

## For parents

- Set rules together and follow them yourself; children copy what they see.
- Use family features (supervised accounts, time limits) as support, not spying.
- Keep conversations open about scams, bullying and pressure online.
