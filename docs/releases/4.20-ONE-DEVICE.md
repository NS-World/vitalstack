# 4.20.0: one device at a time

4.20.0 is 4.19.0 plus the change below. The same change is in 5.0.0-dev.

- **Theme zip:** `vitalstack-theme-v4.20.0.zip` (upload over any 4.12.x – 4.19.x)
- **Whole theme change from 4.12.0:** `docs/releases/4.20.0-hotfix.patch`
- **Plugins:** unchanged (Growth Desk 1.0.0, AdSense Helper 1.0.1, Marketing 1.0.0, Festive 1.0.0).

## What it does

A reader's account is signed in on **one device at a time**. Signing in on a new device signs out
the other devices automatically.

| Where | What the reader sees |
|---|---|
| **New device**, right after signing in | 🔐 **"You're signed in on this device"**: "Your VitalStack account works on one device at a time, so your progress and premium access stay yours. We signed you out on: **Chrome on Windows** (signed in 2 hours ago)." with **Got it, continue** and **Not you? Change your password** |
| **Old device**, page already open | Within about 90 seconds, or as soon as the tab is opened again: 👋 **"You were signed out on this device"**: "Your account was signed in on **Safari on iOS** 1 minute ago…" with **Sign in on this device** and **Wasn't you? Reset your password** |
| **Old device**, next page | The same message once. After that the device is simply signed out |

How it behaves:

- **Signing in again:** a reader can sign in again on the old device, and then the other one is signed out instead. Progress is saved to the account, so nothing is lost.
- **Covers every sign-in:**
  - password with email code;
  - email code only;
  - sign-up;
  - `wp-login.php`.
- **Covers every page:** WordPress's own session is ended, so the old device's cookie stops working everywhere, including the protected premium lessons.
- **Staff are not limited:** anyone who can edit posts can stay signed in on their own devices. A filter (`vitalstack_single_session`) can switch the rule off.
- **With the premium device limit (4.8):** that limit still decides which devices may open premium lessons (one phone and one computer). The new rule makes sure only one of them is signed in at a time.

## UI and accessibility

- **Look:** a centred card on a dimmed, blurred page, in the site's colours, light and dark.
- **Keyboard:** focus starts on the main button and stays inside the card (Tab and Shift+Tab). Escape closes the "signed in here" card.
- **Phones:** fits a 360 px phone. There is no animation when the reader prefers reduced motion.

## Security and privacy

- **What is stored:** only a short device label ("Chrome on Windows") and a time, never the IP address. They are shown only to the account's own devices.
- **Which devices get the explanation:** only sessions this rule ended, matched by the session token's hash. A device the reader signed out by hand from "My devices" is not told it was signed in elsewhere.
- **Checking open pages:** a small `admin-ajax` call from open pages, every 90 seconds while the tab is visible.
  - It is public because a signed-out device has no nonce.
  - It only reads, and it answers only about a sign-in cookie whose signature this site verifies, with the same check WordPress itself uses, including the WordPress 6.8 password-hash rule.
  - Signed-out callers are limited to 30 requests per 10 minutes per IP.
- **Caching:** pages for a device that was just signed out are not cached, and the dead cookie is cleared.
- **A bug caught in testing:** clearing the dead cookie while WordPress was still working out the user made the request loop until it timed out. Cookies are now cleared only after the user is known, with a guard against re-entry. The test that found it is in the suite.
