## 4.13.4: what's new since 4.13.3

1. **🎮 Break button in the header, on every page.** It opens a small "Break zone" panel. On phones
   the button is just the 🎮 icon, and the panel opens as a sheet from the bottom. It has 3 tabs:
   - **Games:** the game of the day as a big card, all 13 brain games, Bug Runner and AI City. One
     tap opens that game straight away.
   - **Focus music:**
     - 4 stations: Lo-fi, Rain, Calm keys and Brown noise, with a volume slider.
     - The music is made live in the browser: no audio files, no streaming, nothing sent anywhere,
       and no copyright issue.
     - Nothing plays until the reader presses Play.
     - While music plays, the header button shows small moving bars. When the reader opens another
       page, the music pauses (browsers don't allow sound to carry over). The button shows a dot,
       and one tap resumes it.
     - Only one sound plays at a time: starting this music stops the lesson "Focus sounds", and
       the other way round.
   - **Focus timer (25 + 5):**
     - The reader starts 25 minutes of study, and the countdown shows on the header button on
       every page.
     - When it ends, a soft chime plays and the panel says "25 minutes of focus done! Take 5: play a
       quick game". A 5-minute break timer follows.
     - This keeps readers on the site for full study sessions, which helps the 30-minute goal.
   - **Tools:** the 10 free tools (Python compiler, SQL playground, HTML editor, JSON, regex, CSV
     and more).
2. **💬 "Ask" assistant, bottom-right on every page.** It answers the common questions instantly,
   in a friendly sales style:
   - **Prices** (all plans), **best plan** (with the real saving maths: all 3 tracks for ₹1,499
     instead of ₹2,997 bought separately, ₹125/month), **offers** (launch prices with the real
     earlier price struck, the invite bonus, coupons), **invites**, **refunds** (honest "all sales
     are final", or your refund window if you set one), **payment** (UPI, cards, Razorpay),
     **devices**, **certificates**, **what's free**, **where a beginner should start**, the tracks,
     ads, games and music, and contact.
   - It understands English and Hinglish ("price kitna hai", "refund milega?", "bore ho raha hu").
   - Typing a course name ("python", "react", "sql") links to that course.
   - For anything else, it offers a site search and the contact page.
   - Prices follow the same rule as the pricing page: **₹ in India, $ everywhere else**. The prices
     come from *Settings → VitalStack Payments*, so the assistant updates when you change them.
   - While **Sales: Open** is unticked, it says "Premium opens soon, join the waitlist".
   - On the pricing page, after 20 seconds, a small bubble asks "Not sure which plan? Ask me" (once
     per visit).
   - The chat is remembered while the tab stays open, across pages.
3. **Security of the assistant (by design):**
   - It is **not connected to any AI service and has no server endpoint**. It runs in the browser
     from public facts only (plan prices, page links, course names).
   - So there is nothing for a hacker or an AI agent to attack, nothing to "prompt-inject", no API
     key to steal and no bill to run up, and it can't invent wrong prices.
   - What a visitor types never leaves their device.
   - Replies are written as plain text, so typed code can never run (tested with an
     `<img onerror>` / `<script>` attempt).
   - No setting, key or private data is in the page: tested.
4. **Offers follow the same currency as the pricing page:** for signed-in readers, the server's
   answer (country from the CDN, then time zone); for visitors, the time zone. India always sees ₹,
   everyone else $.
5. **Fix:** on 320px-wide phones, the home-page sample certificate was 19px too wide and made the
   page scroll sideways. It now fits.

To turn either feature off, a developer can add
`add_filter( 'vitalstack_break_enabled', '__return_false' );` or
`add_filter( 'vitalstack_helper_enabled', '__return_false' );` to a small plugin.

**Why the assistant is not an "AI" (and how to add one later):**
- A real AI chatbot needs an API key on the server, a paid account and a server endpoint that
  anyone on the internet can call. That brings cost per message, abuse and prompt-injection risk.
  It can also invent prices or promises that you would then have to honour.
- For prices, offers and site questions, fixed answers from your own data are safer and always
  correct.
- If you want an AI later, it should go through a rate-limited server proxy, with the key stored
  on the server and answers limited to your site's facts.

   all VitalStack emails as spam, including sign-up codes and receipts, and it could also put the
   AdSense account at risk. The theme keeps its one-click unsubscribe. To keep readers, use the
   ideas in "Keeping readers" below instead.

- On the unsubscribe page, offer "Weekly digest instead" or "Pause for 30 days" before the final
  button.

- "Continue where you left off" at the top of the home page for signed-in readers.
- Short quizzes after each lesson, with XP and a leaderboard (already in the theme), and link the
  next lesson clearly.

**Security (what the theme already does, and what to add on the host):**

- Already in the theme: rate limits on sign-in, sign-up and codes; nonces and capability checks on
  every form and REST route; no cross-site REST access; security headers; temporary-email
  sign-ups refused; AI training crawlers blocked in robots.txt; premium lessons served only to
  owners, with 2-device control; no AI chat endpoint that could be abused.
- **Add on the host (needed: no theme alone can promise "never breached"):**
  - Cloudflare (free plan) in front of the site, with **Bot Fight Mode** and the managed WAF rules
    on;
  - **two-factor login** for every admin account, and strong unique passwords;
  - keep WordPress, plugins and the theme updated, and delete unused plugins;
  - daily backups in hPanel, with one copy kept off the server;
  - hPanel → Security → Malware scanner on.

## 4. Invite friends

**How it works:**

1. Every signed-in reader sees **"🎁 Invite friends, get Pro free"** on their account page. It has
   their link (`https://vitalstack.co.in/?ref=VSXXXXXX`), a Copy button and WhatsApp, Telegram,
   LinkedIn, X, Email and phone-share buttons.
2. **When a friend opens the link:**
   - the code is kept for 30 days;
   - the address is cleaned to the normal page URL, so Google and AdSense see one URL per page;
   - a small note says "A friend invited you! Create a free account, and get 10% off".
3. **When the friend signs up**, they are linked to the inviter. On /pricing/, their 10% discount is
   filled in and applied automatically. It works on their **first purchase only**.
4. **The inviter earns Pro days** on every track:
   - **30 days** for every friend who buys, 3 days after the purchase (in case the payment is disputed);
   - **7 days** for every **3 friends** who sign up **and finish at least one lesson**.
   - This works even while premium is on the waitlist. Free Pro days are great publicity.

**Fraud protection (tested):**

- **Own code:** you can't use your own code ("That's your own invite code…").
- **Disposable inboxes:** about 60 temporary-email services (mailinator, yopmail, …) can't sign up at
  all.
- **Gmail aliases:** `a.b+1@gmail.com` and `ab@gmail.com` count as one person.
- **Real learners only:** a friend counts only after finishing a lesson. Just signing up earns
  nothing.
- **Orders that don't pay out:**
  - refunded orders;
  - practice (demo) orders;
  - purchases from the last 3 days (shown as "pending").
- **No double payouts:** rewards are worked out from the real records each time and can never be
  paid twice, even if two requests arrive at once.
- **Yearly caps:** at most 12 rewards of each kind per reader every 12 months (12 × 30 days is a free
  year of Pro).
- **Sign-up limits:** sign-up is already limited to 10 per IP per hour, with an email code and a bot
  trap.

**Admin:** *Users → Invites*:

- turn the program on or off;
- change the discount %, the days and the caps;
- see the top inviters (friends joined, learning, bought, Pro days given).

## 5. Security: stopping copying

What is already protected (unchanged):

- **Premium lessons:**
  - the text is never in the page source;
  - it loads from the server only for a paying reader, at most 40 lessons an hour and 200 a day;
  - the device limit applies;
  - every copy carries an invisible watermark with the reader's ID, plus a visible one;
  - copying, right-click and printing are off.
- **Forms:** nonce, bot trap and rate limits on sign-up, login, contact and codes. Accounts lock after
  10 wrong passwords an hour.
- **No AI endpoints:** the theme has no AI or LLM endpoint for an "AI agent" to abuse.

New in 4.13:

1. **robots.txt blocks AI training crawlers:**
   - GPTBot, ClaudeBot, CCBot, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended, Bytespider, Meta, Amazonbot,
     PerplexityBot, Diffbot and others (30 names).
   - **Googlebot, Mediapartners-Google (the AdSense crawler), AdsBot and Bingbot are named and
     allowed**, so search traffic and AdSense are not affected.
   - If your host serves a physical `robots.txt` file, the theme can't add these lines. Delete that
     file, or copy the lines from `/robots.txt` on a test copy.
2. **Temporary-email sign-ups are refused** with a clear message.
3. **Copied lesson text gets a source link.** Copying 200+ characters of a free lesson adds
   "Source: VitalStack · <page link>". Code blocks copy exactly as selected, because beginners need to
   copy code. Copied text spreads your link instead of hiding it.

**Honest limit:** any page a browser can show can be copied by a determined person. robots.txt is a
request that big AI companies follow; bad bots ignore it. For real blocking of scrapers, use the free
**Cloudflare** plan in front of the site:

- turn on **"Block AI bots"**, Bot Fight Mode and SSL "Full (strict)";
- turn off the Hostinger CDN when Cloudflare is on.

The theme already reads Cloudflare's visitor IP correctly.

## 6. Razorpay setup from scratch

The payment code is already built and tested: server prices, signature checks, webhook,
refunds/disputes, receipts and GST invoice numbers. You only need an account and keys.

1. **Create the account:** sign up at razorpay.com with your business email and phone.
2. **KYC / activation:**
   - add PAN, bank account and address;
   - add your website `https://vitalstack.co.in`;
   - business category *Education → E-learning*;
   - Razorpay checks that the site has these pages **published**, so publish them before applying:
     - Privacy Policy;
     - Terms;
     - Refund and Cancellation Policy (slug `refund-policy`);
     - Contact Us.
3. **Payment capture: Automatic.** In *Account & Settings → Payment capture*, choose
   **Automatic**. Otherwise payments stay "authorized" and Razorpay refunds them after a few days.
4. **Test keys first:**
   - In *Account & Settings → API Keys*, set the mode to **Test**, then **Generate key**. Copy the
     Key ID (`rzp_test_…`) and Key Secret.
   - In WordPress, go to *Settings → VitalStack Payments*:
     - Mode: **Test**;
     - paste the Test Key ID and Test Key Secret;
     - Save. The secret is stored encrypted.
5. **Webhook:**
   - In *Account & Settings → Webhooks → Add new webhook*:
     - URL: `https://vitalstack.co.in/wp-json/vitalstack/v1/pay/webhook`. The same address is shown
       on the settings page.
     - Secret: make up a long random string.
     - Active events: `payment.captured`, `order.paid`, `payment.failed`, `refund.processed`,
       `payment.dispute.created`.
   - Paste the same secret into **Webhook Secret** in WordPress. Save.
6. **Test purchase:**
   - tick **Sales: Open**;
   - buy Pro Monthly with Razorpay's test card or test UPI (`success@razorpay`);
   - check that access appears on your account page and a receipt email arrives;
   - in Razorpay test mode, refund it and check that access is removed. This tests the dispute and
     mistake path; readers can't ask for refunds.
7. **International payments (needed for $):** in Razorpay, request *International cards*. With
   Currency = Automatic, readers outside India pay in $. Until Razorpay approves international cards,
   their checkout shows "International card payments are not available right now". To sell only in ₹
   meanwhile, choose **₹ only**.
8. **Go live:**
   - after activation, generate **Live** keys and paste them;
   - set Mode: **Live**;
   - repeat step 5 in Live mode (live webhooks are separate);
   - make one real ₹199 purchase and refund it.
9. **While you are not ready to sell:** untick **Sales: Open**. Everyone sees "Join the waitlist".
