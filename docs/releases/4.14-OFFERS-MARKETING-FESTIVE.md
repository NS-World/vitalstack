## 0. 4.14.1: emails going to spam (fix)

Emails land in spam for two kinds of reasons. **Both must be fixed.** The code part is done in
4.14.1; the setup part only you can do in hPanel.

**Done in the theme (every email it sends: sign-up codes, receipts, lesson emails, marketing):**

- **Plain-text part.** Each email now also carries a plain-text version next to the HTML.
  HTML-only mail is a common spam signal.
- **Return-Path.** When no SMTP plugin is used, the envelope sender (Return-Path) now matches the
  From address (`contact@vitalstack.co.in`). Without this, SPF/DMARC alignment fails.
- **Message-ID.** It now uses `@vitalstack.co.in` instead of the server's internal name.
- **Other plugins.** Their emails are not changed.
- **New page: Tools → Email check.** It checks, from your own server:
  - whether an **SMTP plugin** is active;
  - whether the **From address** is on this site's domain;
  - your domain's **SPF**, **DKIM**, **DMARC** and **MX** records.

  Each red item shows the exact fix. The page also has a **Send test** button.
- **Dashboard warning.** Admins see "Your emails may land in spam" on the Dashboard while something
  important is red.

**What you need to do (10–20 minutes, once):**

1. **Create the mailbox:** hPanel → Emails → create `contact@vitalstack.co.in` (or the address in
   *Customize → Contact*).
2. **SMTP plugin:** install **WP Mail SMTP** (or FluentSMTP) → Mailer **Other SMTP**:
   - host `smtp.hostinger.com`, port `465`, encryption `SSL`;
   - username = the full email, password = that mailbox's password;
   - From email = the same address, and tick "Force From Email".

   This is the **biggest single fix**.
3. **DNS records:** hPanel → Emails → your domain → **DNS / Email authentication**. Click "Add" or
   "Fix" for **SPF** and **DKIM**, then add **DMARC**:
   - TXT name `_dmarc`, value `v=DMARC1; p=none; rua=mailto:contact@vitalstack.co.in`.
   - Keep only **one** SPF record (`v=spf1 include:_spf.mail.hostinger.com ~all`).
4. **Check and test:**
   - open **Tools → Email check** and make every line ✅ (DNS can take a few hours);
   - go to **mail-tester.com**, copy its address, use **Send test**, and aim for **9/10 or more**.
5. **Train Gmail:** mark the first few emails as "Not spam" and add the address to your contacts.
   Start marketing slowly (a few dozen a day) for the first two weeks.

**UAT note:** UAT runs on `orchid-toad-489394.hostingersite.com`, but its emails say they come
from `contact@vitalstack.co.in`. Mail providers see that as spoofing, so UAT emails often go to
spam until UAT uses the same SMTP login. That is expected. Test real delivery on PROD after
steps 1–4.

## 1. Offer strip on every page (theme)

A bright red-orange strip above the header, like big hosting sites use for sales:

> 🔥 Launch sale ✦ Pro Yearly **₹1,499** ~~₹2,999~~ **Save 50%** ✦ All 3 career tracks ✦ Just ₹125 a
> month, paid once ✦ 🎁 Invite a friend: they get 10% off ✦ 🎓 Certificates · real projects · no ads
> **[Claim deal →]** ✕

- **Currency:** visitors in India see ₹. Everyone else sees $49 ~~$69~~ Save 29%. This is the same rule
  as the pricing page.
- **Prices:** they come from *Settings → VitalStack Payments*, so the strip changes when you change
  prices.
- **Motion:** the text scrolls slowly and pauses when touched or hovered. Readers with "reduce
  motion" see it still.
- **Who sees it:** premium readers, staff, the pricing page and the account page never show it.
- **Closing:** ✕ hides it for 24 hours.
- **Waitlist mode:** while **Sales: Open** is off, the button says "Join waitlist".
- **Analytics:** links carry `utm_source=site&utm_medium=offer_bar`, so Google Analytics shows how
  many visits and sales the strip brings.
- **Honest:** the struck price is your real earlier price, and there is no fake countdown. This
  matters for AdSense and for Google Ads.

## 2. VitalStack Marketing plugin (emails that bring learners back)

Install: *Plugins → Add New → Upload* `vitalstack-marketing-1.0.0.zip` → Activate →
*Settings → VitalStack Marketing*.

**It is OFF after installing.** Before you switch it on:

1. Install an SMTP plugin (for example "WP Mail SMTP") and connect your Hostinger email
   (`hello@vitalstack.co.in`). Without SMTP, many emails land in spam.
2. Add SPF, DKIM and DMARC records for your domain (hPanel → Emails → your domain shows them).
3. Tick **On**, save, then use **Send me a sample** to see each email in your own inbox.

**Automatic emails** (each can be switched off):

| Email | When | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Welcome: first steps | Day 2 after sign-up | A 15-minute plan: one lesson, the quiz, the 🎮 Break zone |
| Welcome: career tracks + offer | Day 5 | The 3 tracks, projects, certificate, offer box |
| Offer after signing in | 45 minutes after a learner signs in (at most every 14 days) | "Good to see you back", offer box |
| Pricing reminder | 1–3 days after visiting /pricing/ without buying | Honest "how to choose", offer box |
| We miss you | 7 days away | "Your next lesson takes 5 minutes", best streak, game of the day |
| We kept your place | 21 days away | Progress saved, what's new, offer box |
| Still want lessons? | 45 days away | Polite check-in, easy unsubscribe |

- **Campaigns:** write your own email (subject, heading, message, button, optional offer box) and
  send it to:
  - subscribers without Premium,
  - all subscribers, or
  - subscribers away for 14+ days.

  It goes out in small batches every 5 minutes (you choose the speed). **Send me a test** first.
  Premium learners get campaigns without the offer box.
- **Offer box:** a red-orange card with the Pro Yearly price in the learner's currency (₹ or $, saved
  when they sign in), the struck price, the SAVE badge and the per-month price.
- **Stats:** a count per email type, the last 30 sends, and UTM tags on every link
  (`utm_source=email&utm_campaign=<email>`), so Google Analytics shows the visits and sales.

**Rules it always keeps** (these protect your domain and the AdSense account):

- Only learners who **confirmed their email and subscribed** get anything. People who never
  subscribed never get marketing.
- Every email has the theme's **one-click unsubscribe**: the link in the footer, plus the Gmail/Yahoo
  `List-Unsubscribe` header.
- At most **one marketing email every 3 days** per person (you can change this), and at most 150
  automatic emails an hour.
- Offer emails go only to learners **without Premium**. While sales are closed they are skipped,
  unless you tick "send while sales are closed".
- No outside services, no tracking pixels, and no data leaves your site.

**For exact timing**, add a cron job in hPanel (Advanced → Cron Jobs) that runs every 5 minutes:

```
wget -q -O - https://vitalstack.co.in/wp-cron.php?doing_wp_cron >/dev/null 2>&1
```

Then add `define( 'DISABLE_WP_CRON', true );` to `wp-config.php`.

## 3. VitalStack Festive plugin (festival looks)

Install: *Plugins → Add New → Upload* `vitalstack-festive-1.0.0.zip` → Activate →
*Settings → VitalStack Festive*.

On festival days the site dresses up by itself:

- **Greeting band on the home page:** a festival-colour band, for example "🪔 Happy Diwali! May your
  knowledge shine as bright as diyas", with a **Diwali Sale →** button.
  - The button is not shown to premium readers.
  - On phones the button is hidden (the offer strip already has one), so the band stays at two
    lines.
- **Offer strip in festival colours,** led by the festival sale name ("🪔 Diwali Sale").
- **A garland under the header on every page.** Each festival has its own:
  - Diwali, Navratri, Ganesh, Rakhi: marigold beads;
  - Christmas, New Year, Eid, Programmers' Day: twinkling lights;
  - Republic Day and Independence Day: tricolour;
  - Holi and Sankranti: moving colours.
- **Characters** get a small festive item next to them (🪔 🎄 🎨 🇮🇳 🪁 🌙 🌺 …).
- **A 5-second burst** of festive emoji when the home page opens: once per visit, and never with
  "reduce motion".

Festivals included: New Year, Makar Sankranti/Pongal/Lohri, Republic Day, Holi, Eid, Independence
Day, Raksha Bandhan, Ganesh Chaturthi, Programmers' Day (13 Sep), Navratri/Dussehra, Diwali and
Christmas.

- **Dates:** fixed festivals repeat every year. Festivals that move (Holi, Eid, Rakhi, Ganesh,
  Navratri, Diwali) are set for 2027. **Check them each year in Settings** (moon-sighted dates can
  shift by a day).
- **Preview:** each festival has a Preview link. Only admins see it, via `?vs_festive=diwali`.
  Visitors can't switch festivals.
- **Modes:**
  - Automatic (by date);
  - "Always show <festival>", for example to run a sale longer;
  - Never.
- **Greetings and button link:** you can write your own greeting for any festival, and choose where
  the button goes (default: the pricing page).
- **Light:** about 6 KB of CSS and JS, loaded only while a festival is on. No images and no outside
  files.
- **AdSense-safe:** decorations never cover the page for long. The burst ends after 5 seconds, and
  the garland sits under the header.

## 6. Growth plan: from ₹1 lakh to ₹10 lakh a month

These are planning numbers, not promises. Sales follow traffic and trust.

- **The maths:** at about ₹1,400 a sale (Pro Yearly with some coupons):
  - ₹1 lakh a month ≈ **70 sales**;
  - ₹10 lakh a month ≈ **700 sales**.
- **A typical funnel:**
  - 2–3% of visitors make a free account;
  - 2–5% of active accounts buy within 60 days;
  - so ₹1 lakh needs roughly **60,000–120,000 visitors a month**, and ₹10 lakh needs 10× that, or
    better conversion.
- **What moves those numbers, in order:**
  1. **SEO (biggest, free, compounding).** Publish 3–5 lessons or guides a week on search-heavy
     topics: "pandas tutorial", "matplotlib bar chart", "SQL joins", "how to use Google Colab". The
     free tools (Python compiler, SQL playground) already rank-friendly; link them from every
     lesson.
  2. **Short videos.** One 30–60 second reel or short a day on Instagram and YouTube: one concept
     from a lesson's animation, then "free lesson at vitalstack.co.in". Use the posters and the
     social pack. Post the same clip on LinkedIn for the working-professional audience.
  3. **WhatsApp and Telegram community.** A free "Daily Data & AI tip" channel. Link the daily
     challenge and new lessons there, and run festival offers there first.
  4. **Emails (this plugin).** Turn it on once SMTP works. Win-back and pricing reminders usually
     bring 10–30% of email-driven sales.
  5. **Invites.** Ask happy learners to share their invite link (10% for the friend, Pro days for
     them). Feature it in the day-5 email.
  6. **College tie-ups.** Free workshops for clubs, with a group coupon. Students share in batches.
  7. **Paid ads later.** Only after organic conversion is proven: small Google Search budgets on
     "data science course online India", sent to the pricing page with a coupon. Track with UTM
     tags.
- **Weekly check:** in Google Analytics, look at visitors → sign-ups → pricing views → sales. Fix
  the weakest step first.
