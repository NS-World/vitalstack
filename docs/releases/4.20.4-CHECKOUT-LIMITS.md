# VitalStack 4.20.4: "Too many attempts" while testing checkout

## Problem
A reader may create 10 checkout orders an hour (anti-abuse limit). While testing with Razorpay
test keys the owner hit it, and the pricing page showed "Too many attempts". Each try restarts
the one-hour window.

## Fix (new `inc/pay-limits.php`; three limit numbers in `inc/payments.php` now read from it)

| Mode | Orders / hour | Coupon checks / hour | Payment checks / hour |
|---|---|---|---|
| Live | 10 (unchanged) | 30 (unchanged) | 20 (unchanged) |
| Test, reader | 60 | 180 | 120 |
| Test, admin | 1000 | 1000 | 1000 |

A test-mode reader who was already blocked at 10 can try again at once after this update.
Filter: `vitalstack_pay_limit( $limit, $action, $is_test )`.

## Tests
- Unit: test reader 60, admin 1000, live 10/30 unchanged, an already-blocked reader is unblocked.
- Pay unit tests: same result as 4.20.3. Local e2e 25/25.
