## 1. Landing offers, like a big sale (Growth Desk → Offer)

When the offer is on, visitors see the deal the moment they land:

| Where | What |
|---|---|
| **Home page** | A deal card at the top of the hero, beside the headline: price, the regular price struck through, **"Save X%"**, a live **countdown**, 3 benefits, a "Join the waitlist" or "Get Pro Yearly" button, and a secure-checkout line |
| **Pricing page** | A deal panel first: price, saving, countdown, the benefits and **"See all plans ↓"**, before the trailer video. Plan cards also show the struck regular price |
| **Other pages** | A small card that slides in at the bottom left, after 12 seconds or half the page, **once every 3 days** per visitor. It never covers another popup (study buddy, cookie note, dialog) and waits until that one closes. Escape or ✕ closes it |

How it works:

- **Currency:** ₹ in India and $ elsewhere, the same as the pricing page.
- **Who sees it:** premium readers and staff never see the offer. Admins can preview it with "Preview the home page as a visitor".

**Honest by design.** Indian consumer law (the dark-patterns guidelines) bans fake urgency and fake discounts, and so do Google's ad policies. So:

- **Regular price:** the struck-through price and "Save X%" appear only for the regular prices you type in (the real price after the offer). A "regular" price lower than today's price is refused.
- **Countdown:** it appears only when you set a real end date. When that time comes, every offer element disappears by itself, even on pages the cache already served.
- **Benefit lines:** they are counted from the site itself, such as the number of tracks and lessons.

## 2. The agents (Growth Desk → Sales / Marketing / Testing & Security)

The agents are rule-based helpers that run inside WordPress on a schedule:

- no AI service and no outside calls (the site only opens its own pages);
- no public endpoint, so nothing a visitor or a bot can reach or talk into doing something;
- every screen is for administrators only, and every button has a nonce.

| Agent | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Sales** | Paid orders, revenue in ₹ and $, started checkouts and the share paid, failed payments, refunds/disputes, best plans, coupons, the latest 10 orders (buyer emails masked like `a***@g***.com`). Plain-rule tips: sales closed, low paid share, no deadline, no saving shown, failed $ payments |
| **Marketing** | Sign-ups (7/30 days), subscribers and how many are confirmed and reachable, the state of offer emails, festivals coming up (from the Festive plugin) with "plan an offer a week before", a **share kit** of ready-to-post messages with tracked links for WhatsApp, LinkedIn, Instagram, X and Threads, and **find a reader** to subscribe or unsubscribe them by hand |
| **Testing & Security** | Every day: opens the home, pricing, Website Builder and a lesson page; checks sales mode and keys, the payment webhook, email deliverability (SPF/DKIM/DMARC via Tools → Email check), search visibility, the AdSense ID and AdSense Helper results, HTTPS, an administrator called "admin", two-factor sign-in for every admin, too many admins, PHP errors on pages, PHP version, pending updates and the code editor. **It emails you only when something is red.** Every **Monday** you get the week's report: sales, sign-ups, festivals coming up, tips and site health |

## 3. Sign-up: email box ticked by default

- The sign-up form's email box now **starts ticked**. The text is now "Email me new lessons, guides and member offers (unsubscribe anytime)", so new readers join the list unless they untick it.
- **Europe and UK:** visitors there get it unticked, because the GDPR does not accept a pre-ticked box as consent. This uses the visitor's country when the CDN sends it, and their time zone otherwise.
- **Unsubscribing works everywhere:**
  - the account page;
  - the one-click link in every email;
  - Growth Desk → Marketing, where an admin can do it for one reader.

**Why there is no "fake unsubscribe".** A button that says "unsubscribed" but keeps emailing:

- breaks India's DPDP Act (withdrawing consent must be as easy as giving it), the US CAN-SPAM Act and the EU/UK GDPR;
- breaks the Gmail and Yahoo sender rules, which require unsubscribes to be honoured within 2 days.

People who are still emailed after unsubscribing press "Report spam". That ruins the domain's reputation, and then **sign-in codes and receipts go to spam too**. So it would cost sales instead of making them.

## 4. Theme changes

These changes only add things. Without the plugin the site looks the same as before.

- **Home hero:** a wrapper and the `vitalstack_home_hero_aside` hook.
- **Pricing hero:** the `vitalstack_pricing_hero_offer` hook, and `id="plans"` on the plan section so links land on the plans.
- **Sign-up box:** the default (see 3).

## Set it up (after uploading)

1. **Plugins → Add New → Upload** `vitalstack-growth-1.0.0.zip` → Activate. A **Growth Desk** menu appears.
2. **Growth Desk → Offer:**
   - Offer name: for example "Launch Sale".
   - **Offer ends:** a date you will really keep.
   - **Regular prices:** the price each plan will cost after the offer.
   - Save.
3. **Growth Desk → Testing & Security → Run all checks now** and fix anything red.
4. **Growth Desk → Marketing:** copy a share-kit message to your social accounts each day.
