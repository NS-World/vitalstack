# VitalStack digital marketing playbook

**Goal:** turn social media scrollers into followers, then visitors, then free sign-ups, then buyers.
**For:** a busy owner who is not a marketer, with ₹0–₹2,000 a month and about 5–6 hours a week.
**Builds on:** [`GROWTH-PLAN.md`](GROWTH-PLAN.md) (SEO and weekly routine) and
[`SOCIAL-MEDIA-KIT.md`](SOCIAL-MEDIA-KIT.md) (bios, launch posts 1–15). Posts 16–45 and reels 1–5
are in `vitalstack-social-pack-16-45.zip` (not in git). Keep posting those on their schedule; this
playbook adds the **system** around them.

---

## 0. The one idea behind everything

> **The hook is on social media. The payoff is on the site.**

Every post gives real value on its own, so people **save, share and follow**. It also leaves one thing
that can only be *done* on the site: play it, run it, check your answer, take the test, get the
certificate. Reading happens on Instagram. **Doing** happens on vitalstack.co.in.

VitalStack has something most coding pages don't: things people can **interact** with. Social content
should keep showing them off.

| Asset on the site | Why it works on social |
|---|---|
| 60 live code animations | Screen recordings look like magic; nobody else has them |
| 14 brain games (Bug Runner, Big-O Snap…) | "Bet you can't beat 30 seconds" challenge reels |
| 20+ free browser tools (Python compiler, SQL playground, token counter…) | "Ye bhi free hai?!" surprise |
| 22 mock tests + daily challenge + XP streaks | Scores to show off, streaks to protect |
| Indian datasets: UPI Fraud Radar, food-delivery ETA, clean-air alert, Scam SMS Shield, Kirana agent | **Desi data stories nobody else can tell** |
| Crew: Dev, Chip, Bugsy, Byte | Recurring characters = a brand people remember |
| Free certificates + 30-Day Java Interview Plan PDF | Reasons to sign up (sign-up = email = future sale) |

## 3. Six signature series (our unique content)

Pages that grow fast have **recurring series** people come back for, not random posts.
These six are built only from things VitalStack has.

### 🫖 Series 1: "Chai pe Code": tech explained with desi life
The most shareable format for Indian students. One concept, one everyday Indian analogy, 20–30 seconds.

| Concept | Desi analogy |
|---|---|
| API | Dhaba waiter: you don't enter the kitchen, you tell the waiter, the waiter brings the food |
| Cache | Mummy's fridge: leftovers are faster than cooking again |
| Queue (FIFO) | Tatkal ticket line |
| Stack (LIFO) | Plates at a shaadi buffet: last plate on top is picked first |
| Load balancer | Toll plaza opening more lanes |
| Recursion | Two mirrors facing each other at the barber shop |
| Hashing / HashMap | Hostel room numbers: you go straight to room 214, you don't knock on every door |
| Binary search | Finding a word in a dictionary: open the middle, go left or right |
| Big-O | Searching for your friend in a crowd vs. calling their phone |
| Deadlock | Two cars on a narrow gali, both refusing to reverse |
| Git branch | Making a photocopy of your notes before trying something risky |
| Encryption | Writing a secret in a language only you and your best friend know |
| Cookie / session | The token the cloakroom uncle gives you for your bag |
| Database index | The index page at the back of a textbook |
| Async / await | Ordering food on an app and doing homework till it arrives |
| Docker | Tiffin box: same food, any office |
| RAG | Open-book exam: the AI checks your notes before answering |
| LLM hallucination | That one friend who answers confidently even when he doesn't know |
| AI agent | An intern who can use tools: searches, books, then reports back |
| Prompt engineering | Giving clear directions to an auto-wala vs. saying "bas wahi le chalo" |

**Format:** text hook on screen ("API ko samjho dhaba waiter se 🍛") → 3 quick visuals (Canva or
stick figures) → 3-second clip of the matching lesson animation on the site → end card
"Full lesson + live animation: link in bio".
**CTA keyword:** comment **CHAI** → auto-DM with the lesson link (see section 5).
**Site page:** the matching lesson.

### 🐞 Series 2: "Bugsy ki Galti": the crew sitcom
Bugsy (the bug) makes a classic beginner mistake, Byte (the cat) catches it, Dev explains the fix
in one line. Same characters every week = people follow for the characters.
- Episode ideas: `==` vs `.equals()`; list copied by reference; off-by-one loop; forgot `break` in
  switch; SQL `WHERE` missing in `DELETE` (everything gone 😱); password stored in plain text;
  infinite recursion with no base case; `git push --force` on main.
- **Format:** 4-slide comic carousel (Canva, crew PNGs from the theme) or a 15-second reel with
  text bubbles.
- **CTA:** "Bugsy is in the quiz too. Can you beat him? → Daily challenge (link in bio)".

### 🇮🇳 Series 3: "Desi Data": India, in numbers we analysed ourselves
Nobody else has VitalStack's Indian datasets. Turn every signature project into a **story first,
course second**.
- "Which Indian city breathes the worst air in winter? We checked 8 cities with Python 🌫️"
  → Clean-Air Alert project
- "Why does your food arrive late on Friday nights? 🍔" → Food Delivery ETA project
- "How does a bank spot a fake UPI payment in milliseconds? 💸" → UPI Fraud Radar
- "We built an AI that catches scam SMS in Hinglish 📵" → Scam SMS Shield
- "An AI agent that tells a kirana store what to restock 🛒" → Kirana Restock Agent
- **Format:** carousel with one chart per slide (charts from the project lessons), last slide
  "The full project, with code you can run: Data Scientist track".
- **Important:** only post findings that the dataset on the site really shows, and say it is
  VitalStack's practice dataset, not official government data.
- This is the series that **sells the premium tracks** without feeling like an ad.

### 🎬 Series 4: "Dekho Code Chalta Hai": watch the code run
Screen-record a live animation (sorting, recursion tree, React re-render, Spring beans,
attention in transformers). Pure satisfying visuals, perfect for Reels.
- Hook: "Recursion samajh nahi aata? Dekho ye chalte hue 👇"
- Keep it silent-friendly: big captions, trending-style but **original** audio (the pack's music, or
  your own voice).
- **CTA:** comment **PLAY** → link to that lesson so they can press Play with their own input.

### 🧩 Series 5: "Output Batao": daily puzzle + the 60-Second Interview
- **Mon/Wed/Fri:** "What does this print?" (A/B/C/D) on Instagram, Threads, WhatsApp, Telegram.
  The answer goes up **the next day on the site's daily challenge / lesson**, and the story says
  "Answer + explanation 👉 link".
- **60-Second Interview reels:** you (or text on screen) ask one real interview question, pause 3
  seconds ("pause karo, socho"), then show the answer. Ends with "20 more like this: free Java mock
  test".
- Comments are the strongest reach signal; puzzles get the most comments.

### 🎁 Series 6: "Ye Bhi Free Hai?!": the tool reveal
One free tool per week, shown in 15 seconds on a phone: Python compiler with `input()`, SQL
playground, token counter, AI cost calculator, prompt builder, link safety checker, JSON formatter,
regex tester, diagram maker, CSV data explorer…
- Hook: "College lab mein Python install nahi hai? Phone pe chalao 👇"
- **CTA:** comment **TOOLS** → list of all free tools. These pages also rank on Google, so each
  visit has a chance to come back.

### Bonus: "Ek Concept, Teen Level"
Explain one idea three times: to a 10-year-old, to a college student, to a senior engineer.
Great for LinkedIn and YouTube Shorts. Works for recursion, APIs, LLMs, indexes.

## 5. Getting people from the post to the site (the most important section)

Instagram captions can't hold clickable links. These fix that:

### 5.1 Comment-to-DM keywords (biggest lever)
Use **ManyChat** (free plan, Meta-approved) or Instagram's own automated replies. When someone
comments a keyword, they get a DM with the link. This boosts comments (reach) *and* clicks.

| Keyword | Auto-DM sends | Page |
|---|---|---|
| `ROADMAP` | Pick your roadmap (Java, Python, DSA, Data, Gen AI, Agentic AI) | `/roadmaps/` |
| `CHAI` | The lesson from today's Chai pe Code reel | lesson URL |
| `PLAY` | The lesson with the animation | lesson URL |
| `TOOLS` | All free tools | `/tools/` |
| `TEST` | Free mock tests | `/mock-tests/` |
| `PDF` | 30-Day Java Interview Plan (sign-up gift) | `/account/` sign-up |
| `DESI` | The signature project page | track page |
| `AI` | Free "How AI Models Are Built" course + Gen AI roadmap | `/roadmaps/gen-ai-developer-roadmap/` |
| `GAME` | Brain games | `/games/` |

DM template (keep it short and friendly):
```
Hey! 👋 Ye raha tumhara link: <URL>?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=dm&utm_campaign=<keyword>
Free hai, sign-up ke bina bhi chalega. Agar pasand aaye to ek ⭐ reply kar dena 😄
```

### 5.2 UTM on every link
So Google Analytics shows which post brought the visitor. Pattern:
```
https://vitalstack.co.in/<page>/?utm_source=<instagram|youtube|linkedin|whatsapp|telegram|pinterest|reddit>&utm_medium=<bio|dm|story|post|description>&utm_campaign=<series-or-post-name>
```
Example: `https://vitalstack.co.in/tools/sql-playground/?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=story&utm_campaign=free-tool-sql`

### 5.3 Link in bio
One link that opens a short "Start here" list. Until there's a page for it, use
`https://vitalstack.co.in/?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=bio` and put the second link on
`/roadmaps/`. Update the bio link in **every new reel's** first comment ("Link bio mein hai 👆").
(A dedicated `/start/` page on the site with 6 big buttons would raise clicks further; it's a small
theme task for later.)

### 5.4 Pinned posts (top of profile)
1. "Start here: free roadmap for your goal" (comment ROADMAP)
2. Best-performing reel so far (change monthly)
3. "Free vs Pro: what's free forever" (post 36 from the pack)

### 5.5 Owned channels (so we don't depend on the algorithm)
- **WhatsApp Channel** "VitalStack: Daily Coding Question" and a **Telegram channel**. Mention them
  in every 3rd reel ending: "Daily question WhatsApp pe chahiye? Link bio mein".
- **Email:** the site already sends the weekly digest to subscribers. Social → sign-up → email is
  where sales happen. The free certificate and the 30-Day plan PDF are the two sign-up reasons;
  mention one of them every week.

## 6. Reel recipe (every reel, 15–30 seconds)

```
0–2 s   HOOK      Text on screen + first frame that stops the thumb
2–20 s  VALUE     One idea only. Big captions. Something moves every 2 seconds.
20–25 s PROOF     2–3 seconds of the real site (animation, tool, game) on a phone
last 3 s CTA      One action: "Comment ROADMAP" / "Save for exam" / "Link in bio"
```

**Hook bank (rotate these):**
- "Ye galti 90% freshers karte hain 👇" (only if it's a well-known beginner mistake)
- "Interview mein ye pucha gaya. 5 second mein jawab do ⏱️"
- "College ye nahi sikhata, but company pehle din puchti hai"
- "Python install nahi? Koi baat nahi 📱"
- "API ko samjho dhaba waiter se 🍛"
- "Save karo, exam se pehle kaam aayega 📌"
- "Bet you can't score 20/20 😏"
- "ChatGPT andar se kaise kaam karta hai? 30 second mein"
- "Ye website tumhe paise nahi maangti… mostly 😄" (for free vs Pro)

**Make reels without showing your face:** screen recording + text + the pack's original music, or
your voice in Hinglish (voice increases trust and watch time a lot, even without your face).
Tools: **CapCut** (captions, cuts), **Canva** (carousels, comics), phone screen recorder.

## 9. Measuring: the weekly scorecard (10 minutes on Sunday)

Fill this in each week (Google Sheet). Look at **trends**, not single posts.

| Metric | Where | What "good" looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Followers gained (IG, YT, LI, WA) | Each app's insights | Growing every week |
| Reach of reels | IG Insights | Each month's median higher than last month's |
| **Saves + shares** per post | IG Insights | The best sign that content is useful; make more of the top 3 |
| Comments on puzzles | IG | Rising = the algorithm pushes you further |
| Profile visits → link taps | IG Insights | Link taps ÷ profile visits ≥ 5% |
| DM keyword triggers | ManyChat | Which keyword is most used = what people want |
| **Sessions by `utm_source`** | Google Analytics → Acquisition → Traffic acquisition | Social share rising month on month |
| Sign-ups | WordPress → Users / Growth Desk | The real goal of social |
| Sales by source | Orders + GA | Know which platform pays |

**Monthly rule:** find the top 20% of posts (by saves + shares + link clicks), make 3 more like
each, drop the series that came last two months in a row.

## 13. Do / don't

**Do**
- Reply to every comment, especially in the first hour.
- Use original audio (the pack's music or your voice), so posts never get muted or claimed.
- Repurpose winners: a top carousel becomes a reel next month, and a top reel becomes a YouTube long video.
- Credit people when you repost their streaks or scores, and ask first.

**Don't**
- Don't buy followers or use engagement pods: they kill reach and the numbers lie.
- Don't post salary figures, "guaranteed placement" or fake "only 2 seats left".
- Don't use trending copyrighted songs on business accounts.
- Don't use other brands' logos or characters in thumbnails.
- Don't spread across all platforms at full effort. Instagram + YouTube + WhatsApp first; the rest
  is cross-posting.
