Based on the WordPress export dated 2026-09-25 and theme `vitalstack_v2_updated`.
Goal: understand the AdSense "low value content" rejection.

## Most likely reasons for rejection (most important first)

### 1. The news section looks like rewritten content
195 of 273 articles (71% of the site) are news items. Most retell a story
that another outlet broke first: 153 of them link out to an external
source. Almost half are under 600 words. To Google this looks like
**scaled or rewritten content with no added value**, and it is the
textbook cause of a "low value content" rejection. Because it makes up
most of the site, it drags the good posts down with it.

### 2. No clear topic focus
The site covers AI news, Big Tech, gadgets, cybersecurity, apps, startups,
health, mental health, fitness tech, AI careers, AI investing, SaaS ideas,
and Java, React, MySQL and MongoDB tutorials. Neither Google nor a reader
can tell what VitalStack is *the* place for, which hurts ranking,
return visits, and AdSense review.

### 3. Author identities
The export has 12 author accounts (michaelreynolds, sarahmitchell,
jamesthompson, davidharrison, elenabrooks, alexrivera, priyasharma,
nehaSingh, rohanKapoor, vikrammalhotra, ananyapatel, and one email login).
Posts use first-person "I tested this" narratives. **If these are not real
people**, this is a serious trust problem. Google's reviewers and helpful-content
systems look for real, verifiable authors, and made-up personas can breach
AdSense's misrepresentation policy. Use real names only, even if that
means just one author.

### 4. Health content without credentials (YMYL)
There are 36+ posts on health, mental health and digital health. Google
treats health as "Your Money or Your Life" content and holds it to a
higher bar: sources, a named reviewer, and a disclaimer.

### 5. Signs of unedited AI output
- Title: `NLP Basics: How Machines Understand Language (48 characters)`.
  An SEO-tool note was left in the published title.
- Many titles follow the same template: `(2026 Guide)`, `(Step-by-Step Guide)`.

### 6. Thin trust pages
The About page is 44 words and Contact is 36 in the editor. The theme
templates add boilerplate around them, but none of it says **who** runs
the site, where you are, or why you're qualified.

### 7. Theme housekeeping
- `single-tutorials111.php` is a leftover duplicate template.
- `screenshot.png` is 5 MB. It should be about 100 KB (1200×900).
- `Theme URI` points to `vitalstack.io`, not `vitalstack.co.in`.
- `functions.php` is 1,893 lines, so it should be split into `inc/` files.
- Performance, Core Web Vitals and mobile UX need a live-site check. The
  audit environment could not reach the live domain.

## What's already good
- The 10 tutorials are long (1,200–1,600 words) and evergreen. This is the strongest content.
- Some blog posts (AI agents, prompt engineering, 50+ AI shortcuts) have real depth.
- Legal pages exist (Privacy, Terms, Disclaimer, Cookie).
- The theme already has a design system, Article/LearningResource schema, and a cookie banner.
