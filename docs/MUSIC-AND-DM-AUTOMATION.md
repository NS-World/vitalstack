# Music for posts + free comment-to-DM automation

## 1. Music

### Our own tracks (no copyright risk, ever)
Made from scratch for VitalStack (`promo/music/`, script `promo/social-src/make_music.py`):

| Track | Feel | Use for |
|---|---|---|
| `chiptune-levelup.mp3` (132 bpm, 32 s) | Upbeat 8-bit game music | Levels, games, tools, challenges, Spot the Bug reels |
| `lofi-study.mp3` (84 bpm, 32 s) | Chill lo-fi with vinyl crackle | Code in Real Life, roadmaps, cheat sheets, explainers |

Reels Claude makes already have one of these tracks inside. When you upload them, **don't add
Instagram music** on top (or add it at very low volume, see below).

### Instagram's own music (for photo and carousel posts)
On a photo/carousel post: after choosing images, tap **Add music** (the ♫ icon) and pick a track.

- **Business accounts** only get Meta's royalty-free *Sound Collection*. **Creator accounts** get a much
  bigger library. Switching (Settings → Account type and tools → Switch to creator account) is free and
  keeps insights. **Before switching, check the DM automation below still works** (it needs the
  account linked to the Facebook Page); if it stops, switch back.
- In the music picker, prefer tracks with the **↗ trending arrow**: rising sounds get extra reach.
- Pick a 15–30 s clip that starts on a beat, not on silence (fast scrollers decide in about 1 second).

**Mood guide: search these words in the music picker**

| Post format | Search words | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Code in Real Life, roadmaps, cheat sheets | `lofi`, `study`, `chill beats` | Calm = people stay and read every slide |
| Spot the Bug, puzzles | `suspense`, `ticking`, `thinking` | Timer feeling = they try to solve it |
| Code Rizz, memes, Delulu vs Solulu | trending pop (↗), `funny`, `romantic` | Trend audio + humour = shares |
| Levels, games, tools, challenges | `8 bit`, `arcade`, `video game` | Matches the game look |
| Motivation, Main Character Dev | `phonk`, `epic`, `confident` | Gen Z "main character" energy |

### Riding a trend on a reel without copyright problems
Upload our reel (it has our music) → **Add audio** → choose a trending song → set *its* volume to
10–20% and keep the original audio at 100%. The trend boosts reach; our track stays the main sound.
Never put a downloaded MP3 of a commercial song **inside** the video file: business accounts get
muted or blocked for that.

## 2. Comment → automatic DM with the link (free)

### Best free option: Meta Business Suite "comment to message" (built by Meta)
Free, no contact limit, no branding. Needs: Instagram **professional** account linked to the Facebook Page.

1. On a laptop, open **business.facebook.com** → choose the VitalStack Page.
2. **Inbox** → **Automations** (top right).
3. **Create automation** → choose the comment option (named like *"Comment to message"* or
   *"Keywords on posts"*; Meta renames these sometimes).
4. Platform: **Instagram**. Choose **all posts** or specific posts.
5. Keywords: add `LEVEL`, `API`, `BUG`, `TOOLS`, `ROADMAP`, `GAME`, `TEST`, `QUIZ`. Meta allows only a
   few per automation, so make one automation per keyword (each with its own link).
6. Message: paste the DM text (below). Save and turn it on.
7. Test: comment the keyword from a friend's account. The DM should arrive within a minute.

Notes: Meta can send **one private reply per comment**. If a template automation and a custom keyword
automation both match, only the template one is sent, so keep only the custom ones on.

### "Only if they follow" (follow-gate)
- Meta's free tool **can't check** whether the person follows you.
- ManyChat's free plan is tiny (about 25 active contacts a month, "Powered by ManyChat" footer); one
  good post uses that up in a day. Its follower check, if you need it later, is on paid plans
  (from about $14–29 a month). Check the plans page before paying.
- **Recommendation: don't gate.** A gate makes people work before they see value, so fewer comment, and
  fewer comments means less reach. Instead, ask nicely inside the DM (below). Real follows come from
  the *next* post being good.

## 3. Built for fast scrollers (Gen Z, about 1 second per post)

1. **Slide 1 must work with zero reading**: one big question or a 5-word claim, and one visual.
2. **Show progress**: numbers (1/4), level bars, "swipe to start". People finish what they started.
3. **One idea per slide**, 12 words max on most slides.
4. **Give them a role**: "comment your level", "rate 1–10", "tag your Java friend".
5. **Music on every post**, starting on a beat.
6. **Stories every day (3 frames)**: ① the new post with "New post 👆" ② a poll/quiz/slider sticker
   ③ a link sticker to the page. Stickers get taps, and taps tell Instagram to show your posts to more people.
